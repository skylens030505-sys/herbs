1. Who We Are

Herbify is developed and operated by Daison Systems, based in Davao, Philippines. We are the data controller responsible for your personal information under the Philippines Data Privacy Act of 2012 (Republic Act No. 10173).

Contact: herbify.apps@gmail.com

2. What Information We Collect

We only collect information necessary to make Herbify work. We do not sell your data and we do not use it for advertising.

2.1 Information you provide directly

  • Email address — to create and access your account
  • Display name (optional) — shown next to your community posts
  • Account password — stored securely (hashed), never in plain text
  • Community posts — testimonies, comments, ratings, and likes you create
  • Saved herbs — herbs you bookmark in the app

2.2 Information collected automatically

When you sign in, our system records a unique user ID, your sign-up date, and your last sign-in date. We do not collect your IP address, device fingerprint, advertising ID, location, or browsing behavior.

2.3 Information from third-party sign-in

If you use Google Sign-In or Apple Sign-In, we receive only your email address, your name (if you choose to share it), and a unique account identifier. We do not access your contacts, calendar, photos, or any other data.

3. How We Use Your Information

We use your information only for these purposes:

  • To operate the app — show your saved herbs, display community posts, enable interactions
  • To authenticate you — verify your identity at sign-in
  • To provide support — respond to your questions and reports
  • To improve the app — review aggregated usage patterns (not individual tracking)
  • To enforce our Terms — investigate violations and remove rule-breaking content
  • To comply with the law — respond to valid legal requests when required
What we will never do: sell your data, use it for advertising, or share it for marketing purposes.

4. Where Your Data Is Stored

Your data is stored on Supabase, our backend service provider. Supabase uses industry-standard security including encryption in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and encryption at rest.

Supabase servers may be located in the United States or other countries outside the Philippines. By using Herbify, you consent to your data being transferred to and stored in these locations.

Supabase's privacy practices: https://supabase.com/privacy

5. Sharing of Information

We do not sell, rent, or trade your personal information. We share information only in these limited situations:

  • With Supabase — to store and retrieve your account and content
  • With Google or Apple — only when you use their sign-in option
  • With other Herbify users — anything you post in the Community section is publicly visible
  • When legally required — in response to valid court orders or government requests under Philippine law
  • To protect safety — when necessary to protect life, prevent fraud, or prevent serious harm

6. Public Content

Anything you post in the Community section — testimonies, comments, ratings, and likes — is public within the app. Other users can see your display name, the herb you wrote about, your rating, your story, and your comments.

Please don't share sensitive info: Avoid posting full names, addresses, phone numbers, or specific medical diagnoses in community posts.

7. Your Rights

Under the Philippines Data Privacy Act of 2012, you have the right to:

  • Access — request a copy of your personal data
  • Correct — fix inaccurate or incomplete information
  • Delete — delete your account and personal data
  • Object — object to certain types of processing
  • Data portability — get your data in a machine-readable format
  • Withdraw consent — at any time, where processing is based on consent
  • File a complaint — with the National Privacy Commission

How to exercise your rights

Email us at herbify.apps@gmail.com. We respond within 30 days. You can also delete your own community posts and comments directly within the app at any time.

Account deletion

To permanently delete your account and all associated data, email us at herbify.apps@gmail.com from the email address registered to your account, with the subject line "Account Deletion Request". We will delete your account and personal data within 30 days, except where retention is legally required.

8. Data Retention

  • Account data — kept until you request deletion or close your account
  • Community posts — kept until you delete them or delete your account
  • Backups — purged within 90 days after account deletion

9. Children's Privacy

Herbify is not intended for users under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If we learn that we have collected information from a child under 13, we will delete it as quickly as possible.

If you believe a child under 13 has provided us with personal information, please contact us at herbify.apps@gmail.com.

10. Security

We take reasonable measures to protect your information:

  • HTTPS/TLS encryption for all data in transit
  • Encrypted storage at our backend provider
  • Password hashing (passwords are never stored in plain text)
  • Row-level security policies that prevent users from accessing each other's private data

However, no method of internet transmission or electronic storage is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If we become aware of a data breach affecting your personal information, we will notify you and the National Privacy Commission as required by Philippine law.

11. Health and Medical Information Disclaimer

Herbify provides general information about traditional herbal remedies for educational purposes only. It is not a medical app, and the information it provides is not medical advice.

Any health-related information you choose to share in your community posts is voluntarily disclosed by you and becomes public. We treat your community posts as user-generated content, not as confidential health records.

Always consult a qualified healthcare professional before using any herbal remedy — especially if you are pregnant, breastfeeding, taking medication, or have a medical condition.

12. Third-Party Services

We do not use advertising networks, analytics services, crash reporting services, or any third-party trackers.
